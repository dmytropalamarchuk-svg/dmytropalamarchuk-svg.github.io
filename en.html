How I work

Whatever you come with — a building, a municipality or an energy community — the sequence is the same, and you have one counterpart. When an engineer, lawyer or designer is needed, I bring in colleagues from the NGO E-TEAM team or specialists I have worked with on HOA projects.

  1. 1

    Understanding the question

    What exists now, what the concern is, which data are available and which are missing. After the first conversation you will know whether it is worth going further.

  2. 2

    Calculating

    Options, indicative cost, savings and payback. For HOAs I also calculate the financial result per flat, not just for the building.

  3. 3

    Finding funding

    Energy Efficiency Fund programmes, donor grants, loans, local co-financing programmes, the municipal budget.

  4. 4

    Guiding the procedures

    We prepare general meeting or council decisions, applications and reports. Up to verification I advise the HOA head, the project manager and the contractor.

Who does what

Decisions are taken by co-owners or councillors. The design is prepared by a designer, works are done by a contractor, quality is checked by technical supervision. I advise at every stage and make sure documents meet the programme requirements.

For HOAs

What a thermal camera shows

A thermal camera colours warm parts of a facade yellow and orange. It shows where a building loses heat: panel joints, windows, balconies. After insulation the facade looks “colder” on the image because the heat stays inside. That is usually where the conversation about which measures are worth financing begins.

What to prepare for the first conversation

With this information the first consultation is shorter and more useful. Not everything is required — bring what you have.

Homeowner association

  • Heat bills or consumption data for the last 2–3 heating seasons
  • Building passport: year built, storeys, floor area, number of flats
  • Minutes of meetings on renovation, if any
  • Energy certificate or energy audit, if available
  • Photos of the facade, basement and heating substation

Municipality

  • Energy consumption of public buildings for the last 3 years
  • Current energy efficiency programmes, SECAP or energy plan, if any
  • Contact of the responsible person: energy manager, housing or economic department
  • List of projects the municipality is already considering

Community group

  • Members of the group and who intends to join
  • Possible site: roof, land plot, boiler house
  • Approximate annual electricity or heat use of members
  • Position of the local authority
