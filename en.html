How I work

Whatever you come with, the path is the same. You deal with one person; I bring in the engineers, lawyers and finance specialists I have worked with for years when the task needs them.

  1. Understand the question

    What exists today, what the problem is, which data are available and which are missing.

  2. Run the numbers

    Baseline, options, costs, savings and payback.

  3. Find the financing

    Energy Efficiency Fund programmes, donor grants, loans, municipal budgets.

  4. Deliver the result

    General meetings, applications, contractors, verification, reporting.

Prepare for the first conversation

A first consultation is shorter and more useful when you have this information at hand. Bring what you have.

Homeowner association

  • Heat bills or consumption data for the last 2–3 heating seasons
  • Building passport: year, storeys, floor area, number of flats
  • Minutes of meetings on renovation, if any
  • Energy certificate or energy audit, if available
  • Photos of the facade, basement and heating substation

Municipality

  • Energy consumption of public buildings for 3 years
  • Current energy efficiency programmes, SECAP or energy plan, if any
  • Contact of the responsible person: energy manager, housing or economic department
  • List of projects the municipality is already considering

Community group

  • Members of the group and who intends to join
  • Possible site: roof, land plot, boiler house
  • Approximate annual electricity or heat use of members
  • Position of the local authority
